Meet the crew

The people behind Golden Helmets & Green Jackets — on the mic and behind the scenes.

Michael Maier, M.D. “GolfDoc”

Host · Founder

Michael Maier, M.D. — “GolfDoc” to the show — is an orthopedic surgeon and has been an Assistant Professor of Orthopedic Surgery at Baylor College of Medicine since January 2018. A cum laude graduate of the University of Notre Dame (Class of 1994), he earned his M.D. at UT Southwestern Medical School and trained in general and orthopaedic surgery at Duke University Medical Center. Board certified by the American Board of Orthopaedic Surgery, he cares for athletes and active patients across sports medicine, joint reconstruction, trauma and golf-specific injuries, and holds Titleist Performance Institute Medical Level 3 certification. On Golden Helmets & Green Jackets he brings the operating-room view to college football, golf and the Notre Dame stories he has loved since his days under the Golden Dome. Go Irish!

Tevon Coney

Co-host · Former Notre Dame football player

A former Notre Dame linebacker who went on to the NFL, Tevon was the show’s very first guest and has been part of the team ever since — returning for the Michigan State–Notre Dame episode and joining GolfDoc to interview Purdue brothers Steve and John Jackson.

Tevon brings a player’s perspective on injury, resilience, the Notre Dame alumni network and passing the game’s lessons on to the next generation of young athletes.

From West Point to the show

John Robb

USMA Class of 1991 · Army linebacker

John grew up in Houston and didn’t know what West Point was until his junior year of high school, when Army coach Bob Sutton came to recruit him. He played linebacker for Jim Young and Bob Sutton and co-captained the 1990 Army team that beat Navy 30–20.

Today he is founder and managing director of The Stone Creek Group, a Denver wealth-management firm built on “Duty. Honor. Family.” He spent 2001–2015 at Bernstein, has served on the West Point Association of Graduates board and Investment Committee, and earned his MBA at Our Lady of the Lake University.

Otto Leone

USMA Class of 1991 · Former Army quarterback

Otto earned his engineering degree at West Point and quarterbacked Army from 1987 to 1990 in Jim Young’s wishbone. On Halloween 1987 he started the year as sixth-string QB, came in against Temple, and his only pass went 64 yards to Mark Charette for a touchdown in a 17–7 Army win.

He later earned his MBA at Notre Dame, spent 2009–2024 at Schindler Elevator, finishing as VP of the South Central region, and is now SVP, Multi-Family, at Repipe Specialists. His advice to West Point’s Class of 2029: “Be a great teammate, be a servant leader, take care of each other, and always put the mission first.”

Friends of the show

Dr. Tom Parr

Dr. Thomas J. “Tom” Parr, M.D., F.A.C.S., is a 1967 West Point graduate and Army Ranger who commanded an armored cavalry troop in Germany and a mechanized infantry company in Vietnam, then became an orthopaedic surgeon and retired from the Army Medical Corps as a Colonel in 1987. He has practiced orthopaedics in Sugar Land for more than 30 years, now with Baylor St. Luke’s Medical Group, where he is GolfDoc’s partner, and has served on the board of the West Point Society of Greater Houston since 1989. Tom and his wife Joannie brought John Robb and Otto Leone into GolfDoc’s life. Episode 10 is dedicated to Tom, Joannie and Bill York.
