Episode 10: "Life is more than NIL" : West Point Football with Otto Leone and John Robb

West PointArmy footballArmy–Navy

Guest: Otto Leone & John Robb (West Point Class of 1991, Army football)

Notre Dame is on its bye week and Navy is next on Halloween, so GolfDoc hears from the other side of the Service Academy family. Otto Leone, Army quarterback (one-for-one, 64 yards vs. Temple), Ranger School grad and Notre Dame MBA, and John Robb, Army linebacker, 1990 co-captain and founder of The Stone Creek Group in Denver, cover calculus class before kickoff at Michie Stadium, plebe boxing, why Army–Navy is unlike any rivalry, a Desert Storm senior year, Champions Golf Club and Jackie Burke, the West Point network, and the advice they'd give a recruit choosing between NIL money and West Point.
