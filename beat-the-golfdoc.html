How it works

  1. Pick each of GolfDoc's 10 games against the spread (1 point each; Game 10 is the Bonus Game).
  2. Agree or disagree on the Fairway Finder (6 pts), GolfDoc Special (11 pts) and Golden Domer (23 pts) parlays. Agree scores if the parlay hits; Disagree scores if it misses. Max 50.
  3. Picks lock before the first kickoff. Check the live leaderboard to see if you beat the GolfDoc.
