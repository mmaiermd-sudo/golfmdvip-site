Beat the GolfDoc

Think you can out-pick the GolfDoc? Each week, take GolfDoc's 5-game college football slate against the spread, then make your bonus calls on the Fairway Finder, GolfDoc Special and Golden Domer parlays, Cade's Turkey Triple, and the Matty Ice Lock. It's free to play, for bragging rights only.

Winners will be considered for guest cameo appearances on future episodes with the GolfDoc, Cade, Matty Ice and Brandon.

See GolfDoc's Weekly Picks

How it works

  1. Open the entry form and pick each of the 5 games against the spread.
  2. Make your bonus calls: Fairway Finder, GolfDoc Special, Golden Domer, Cade's Turkey Triple and the Matty Ice Lock.
  3. Submit before kickoff, then check the leaderboard to see if you beat the GolfDoc.
