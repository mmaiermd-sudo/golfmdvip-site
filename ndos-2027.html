The game

Auburn comes to South Bend

Notre Dame hosts Auburn on Saturday, Sept. 25, 2027, at Notre Dame Stadium. It will be the first-ever meeting between the Irish and the Tigers, and Auburn’s first game in the state of Indiana. The teams play the return game in Auburn on Oct. 28, 2028. source Auburn schedule

Auburn will be the 13th SEC program Notre Dame has faced. Kickoff time and TV are TBA. source
