NDOS 2025 · USC weekend

29th Annual Notre Dame Orthopaedic Symposium

Oct. 16–18, 2025 · South Bend · No. 13 Notre Dame 34, No. 20 USC 24 · Sat., Oct. 18, 2025

All years202720262025
The meeting

29th annual symposium

The 29th Annual Notre Dame Orthopaedic Symposium ran Oct. 16–18, 2025, in South Bend, the same weekend Notre Dame hosted USC. source

Dr. Chip Taunt, our Episode 5 guest, moderated the hip and knee reconstruction section for the seventh year. He wrote that the speakers “knocked their talks out of the park,” naming Herrick Siegel, Charlie DeCook, Sean Toomey, Richard Illgen and Chad Mather, plus an inspiring talk from Anthony Romeo. source

The game

Irish 34, Trojans 24

On a rain-soaked Saturday night in a sold-out Notre Dame Stadium, No. 13 Notre Dame beat No. 20 USC 34–24. source USC recap

  • Jeremiyah Love ran for a career-best 228 yards and a touchdown, the 18th 200-yard rushing game in program history. source
  • Jadarian Price added 87 rushing yards and a score. Fifteen seconds after USC took a 24–21 lead, his 100-yard kickoff return put the Irish back in front. source
  • The defense forced three second-half turnovers. Captain Adon Shuler forced a fourth-quarter fumble and led the team with 10 tackles, and Luke Talich’s interception sealed it. source
Featured attendees

Friends in South Bend

Dr. Richard C. “Chad” Mather III, MD, MBA

Hip arthroscopy · Duke Orthopaedics · Optum

Dr. Mather is a Duke orthopaedic surgeon whose clinical practice focuses on hip arthroscopy, including femoroacetabular impingement (FAI) and extra-articular hip endoscopy. He is a health-services researcher and decision scientist at Duke, and a former vice chair of practice transformation in the Duke Department of Orthopaedic Surgery.

He also serves as Chief Medical Officer of Optum Specialty Care. He earned his M.D. and M.B.A. at Duke, where he also completed his orthopaedic residency, and he is a past president of the North Carolina Orthopaedic Association.

He was one of the featured speakers at the 2025 NDOS meeting, and GolfDoc was proud to bring him to South Bend.

Sources: Duke Orthopaedics · Optum Health Education · 2025 speaker list (Chip Taunt)

Dr. Jason L. West, MD

General & trauma surgeon · San Antonio, Texas

Dr. West is a board-certified general surgeon in San Antonio, affiliated with Methodist Hospital Stone Oak and Methodist Physician Practices. His practice includes trauma surgery, surgical critical care and acute care (emergency general) surgery.

He earned his M.D. at UT Southwestern Medical School, where he and GolfDoc were medical-school classmates, and completed his surgery residency at LSU Health Shreveport. He is a Fellow of the American College of Surgeons.

Sources: U.S. News · Doximity · WellMed directory

Dr. Jeff Yergler, MD

Hip & knee replacement surgeon · South Bend Orthopaedics

Dr. Yergler is a board-certified orthopaedic surgeon at South Bend Orthopaedics who specializes in minimally invasive hip and knee replacement, including robotic and outpatient joint replacement. A South Bend native, he is continuing a family legacy: his father was one of the original founders of South Bend Orthopaedics.

He graduated from the University of Michigan, earned his M.D. at Indiana University School of Medicine, completed his orthopaedic residency at Duke University Medical Center and an adult reconstruction fellowship at Rush. He is a developer for Zimmer Biomet and Canary Medical (the Persona IQ “smart knee”) and a consulting physician for the University of Notre Dame.
